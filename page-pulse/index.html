Polls, ratings and RSVPs, right on the page.

Ask a question where the work already lives. Page Pulse keeps the question, the responses and the results inside Confluence — no form link, no chat poll, no spreadsheet to chase.

Poll

Two to ten options, single or multi-select, with an optional close date. Show results always, after a person votes, or after the poll closes.

Rating

A simple 1–5 star scale with an average and a distribution, useful for retros, reviews and training feedback.

RSVP

Going, Maybe or Can't go, with an optional capacity. When the event is full, new “Going” responses are declined.

What stays in Confluence

Every mode supports one response per person, a change or remove action, optional name hiding and a CSV download. A byline item shows the total number of responses collected on the page, so owners can see participation without opening the macro.

Runs on Atlassian. Responses are stored in Forge KVS on your Atlassian site. The app does not call an external server, use runtime AI, or collect analytics or advertising identifiers.

How it works

  1. Open a Confluence page and insert /Page Pulse.
  2. Choose Poll, Rating or RSVP, write the question and set the options.
  3. Save the macro, publish the page, and let people respond where they already are.
