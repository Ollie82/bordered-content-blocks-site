Support

Page Pulse is a free app maintained on a best-effort basis. Security issues and data-loss risks are prioritized. There is no guaranteed response time.

Contact

What to include

  • Confluence site URL and page ID
  • Pulse mode (poll, rating or RSVP) and app version
  • Steps to reproduce, expected and actual result
  • Browser and version, light or dark mode
  • Screenshot or short recording, without sensitive content

Common questions

Can I change a question after people voted?

Editing the question or options starts a new result set. The old responses remain in storage but are no longer shown for the new pulse. Changing the close date, visibility or name settings keeps existing responses.

Why does the byline count more than this poll?

The byline counts all Page Pulse responses collected on the page, including earlier versions of a pulse.

Can I export the responses?

Yes, when results are visible to you, use Download CSV. If your browser blocks the download inside Confluence, open the CSV outside the page or contact support.

Are voter names stored?

No. The app stores the Atlassian account ID, the choice and a timestamp. Display names are read from Confluence when results are shown and are not stored.

How do I remove a response?

Open the pulse and choose Remove next to your response. Page owners can ask the publisher to remove a response.

Why can't I vote on a page?

Your Confluence access controls whether you can read the page and respond. If the page is restricted, ask the page owner for access. If you can read the page but still cannot vote, reload and try again; if it persists, contact support with the page ID and the time of the attempt.

Security reports

Use the private security contact on the Marketplace listing; do not post exploit details publicly. You can also email atlassian@foxmail.com with “security” in the subject.

Feature requests

Describe the page pattern and the decision you are trying to support, not only a UI control. Example: “We run a weekly design review and need to collect a 1–5 score plus one comment” or “We need a poll that closes when the page is published.”
