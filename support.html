Community thread: How do you add a border to a coloured panel in Confluence Cloud?

Do not post credentials, page content or vulnerability details in the public thread.

What to include

  • Confluence site URL and page ID
  • Macro name and app version
  • Steps to reproduce, expected and actual result
  • Browser and version, light or dark mode
  • Screenshot or short recording, without sensitive content

Known behavior

In a Live doc, the native-body panel hides its frame while Confluence is editing the body. Use Bordered panel (live edit) if you need the frame visible while typing.
