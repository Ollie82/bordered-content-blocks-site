Bordered Content Blocks · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy applies to the free Bordered Content Blocks app for Confluence Cloud, published by Ollie Guan as an individual developer.

Who we are

Publisher: Ollie Guan, independent developer. Contact: atlassian@foxmail.com.

What the app does

The app provides content blocks (panels, cards, metadata and steps) and a page-level Panel Migrator. The 1.0 release stores block configuration inside the Confluence page's macro data. It does not use an app-level database, external server or analytics service.

Data the app processes

  • Block configuration: title, icon, tone, layout and content for the blocks you insert. This is stored in the Confluence page content, under the customer's control.
  • Page content for migration: when you run Panel Migrator, the app reads the page's ADF body and writes a converted version back to the same page, creating a normal page version.
  • Support communications: if you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send (for example, a page URL, screenshots and a description of the issue).

The 1.0 public app does not store data in an app-level database or Forge KVS, send page or user data to an external server, use runtime AI or LLM services, or collect analytics, advertising identifiers or user profiles.

Why we process it

To render and configure the blocks you insert, to perform the page conversion you explicitly start, and to answer support requests you send us.

Retention and deletion

The app does not maintain a separate copy of page content. The customer controls page retention and deletion through Confluence. Support emails are retained only as long as needed to resolve the request, unless a longer period is required by law.

Sharing

The app does not sell data or share it with third parties. Atlassian hosts the Forge runtime and Confluence content under its own terms and privacy policy. Support email is handled by the email provider used by the publisher.

Security

Access to page content follows the user's Confluence permissions. The app requests only the scopes listed in its Marketplace Privacy & Security tab. No external egress is configured.

Your choices

You can remove a block or the app at any time. Removing the app does not delete page content that you created; delete the relevant page content or contact atlassian@foxmail.com for help.

Changes

We will update this policy if the app's data handling changes and will post the new date here.
